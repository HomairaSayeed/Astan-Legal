# Astan – Child Safety Standards

**Effective Date:** June 2026

At Astan, protecting children and young people is a core priority. We are committed to preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) and maintaining a safe environment for all users.

## Age Requirements

Astan is intended for users aged **13 years and older**. Users under the minimum age requirement are not permitted to create accounts or use the platform.

## Prohibited Content and Behavior

Astan strictly prohibits:

- Child sexual abuse material (CSAM)
- Sexual exploitation of minors
- Grooming or attempting to exploit minors
- Solicitation of sexual content involving minors
- Sharing, promoting, or distributing CSAE-related content
- Any activity that endangers children

Any content or account found violating these standards may be removed immediately.

## Reporting and Moderation

Users can report content, projects, posts, comments, messages, images, videos, or accounts that may violate these standards.

Reports are reviewed and appropriate action may include:

- Content removal
- Account suspension
- Permanent account termination
- Reporting to relevant authorities when required by law

## Safety Measures

Astan maintains measures designed to help protect users, including:

- Community reporting tools
- Content moderation processes
- User account controls
- Enforcement of platform policies

## Cooperation With Authorities

Where legally required, Astan may cooperate with law enforcement agencies and relevant child protection organizations regarding credible reports of child exploitation or abuse.

## Policy Updates

These Child Safety Standards may be updated periodically. Continued use of Astan after updates constitutes acceptance of the revised standards.
